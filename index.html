What to check before ordering

  1. Confirm the variant and size

    For shoes, compare your foot length with the seller’s size chart. For hoodies, measure the chest width, body length, and sleeves of a garment that fits. Save the exact color and size alongside the source listing.

  2. Check warehouse photos

    When QC photos are available, match the size label and color to your order. Check both sides of a pair of shoes and look at the seams, cuffs, and measurements on clothing. Check your agent’s return window before approving shipment.

  3. Compare the delivered cost

    Add the item price, domestic delivery, any agent fees, international shipping, and applicable taxes. Check whether your shipping line charges by actual or dimensional weight, especially if you plan to keep the shoe box.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list used to organize shopping finds. This page shows a selection of HipobuyFind listings with product names, categories, item IDs, and price snapshots.

Can I paste a HipobuyFind item ID into OrientDig?

The ID shown here identifies the HipobuyFind listing. Open the product page and check for the original seller URL. If you find one, check whether OrientDig supports that marketplace and the specific item before ordering.

Where can I find QC photos for my order?

Check your agent’s warehouse order page after the item arrives. Photo availability and measurement requests depend on the agent and service selected. A listing photo shows the seller’s presentation of the product, while warehouse photos can show the specific item received.
